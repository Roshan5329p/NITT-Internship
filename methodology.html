Ti-6Al-4V Additive Manufacturing Physics & Validation Methodology

Traceable thermal physics, Bontha solidification mapping, Leave-One-Paper-Out (LOPO-CV) calibration, and ASTM F2924 / F3001 qualification guardrails.

1. Analytical & Hybrid Melt Pool Kinematics

Melt pool geometry, solidus cooling rates (∂T/∂t), and keyhole thresholds combine Rosenthal (1946) point-source heat conduction with the KU Leuven hybrid formulation (Coen et al., 2022) and King et al. (2014) normalized enthalpy criterion (ΔH/hs).

  • LPBF & L-DED Regimes: Separates lack-of-fusion, conduction mode, and keyhole depression by volumetric energy density (VED), beam spot diameter, and melt pool aspect ratio (d/W).
  • WAAM & Binder Jetting: Evaluates linear heat input (J/mm) and coarse prior-β overheating for arc deposition, and binder saturation + vacuum sintering density for Binder Jetting.

2. Bontha 2D/3D Solidification Microstructure Mapping

Thermal gradient G (K/cm) and solidification growth velocity R (cm/s) are solved from surface (z = 0) to melt pool root (z = zm) across 2D thin-wall and 3D bulky half-space formulations (Bontha et al., 2006, 2009), classifying columnar, mixed, and equiaxed prior-β grain growth via Hunt's CET boundaries.

3. LOPO-CV Machine Learning & PINN Calibration

All predictions are validated using strict Leave-One-Paper-Out Cross-Validation (LOPO-CV) across 39 verified bulk Ti-6Al-4V studies extracted from 199 indexed publications, with calibrated 95% empirical prediction intervals and Mahalanobis Out-of-Distribution (OOD) interval widening.

  • LPBF Benchmark Accuracy: 97.7%–98.5% accuracy on strength and Vickers hardness, 0.57 percentage points MAE on elongation, and physics-regularized Hall-Petch + autograd PINN residuals.
  • ASTM Qualification Guardrails: Evaluates Rp0.2 yield strength, UTS, ductility elongation, relative density, interstitial oxygen (Grade 5 vs. Grade 23 ELI), and residual stress against ASTM F2924, ASTM F3001, AMS 4999, and ISO 5832-3.
